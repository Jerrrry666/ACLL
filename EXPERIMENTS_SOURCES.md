clients, including self; protected suffixes stay local. All effective
components continue training. Equal training sizes give uniform weights.
Each performance run evaluates personalized post-aggregation R9 adapters on
1600 generated test examples (200/client); PPL covers 800 examples (100/client).
The plot uses full-precision saved `eval_progress.json` client ROUGE-L scores,
not the rounded evaluation CSV or a renamed F1/EM value.

| Total rank | Clients | s0 ROUGE-L (%) | s2 minus s0 (pp) | s3 minus s0 (pp) | s8 minus s0 (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 4 | 64.258432 | +0.098246 | −0.164686 | −0.428953 |
| 64 | 4 | 65.339859 | +0.169271 | +1.016174 | +0.359510 |

The overall equal-client mean is 64.799145% at s0 and 64.764424% at s8,
a −0.034721 pp change. Here the two rank groups contribute equally because
each contains four clients. Their observed peaks occur at s2 and s3,
respectively; these descriptive test peaks do not establish optimal widths.
Rank groups also have different task mixtures, so the contrasts are not causal rank effects.

The cosine gap is **same-rank/different-domain minus
same-domain/different-rank** mean effective-upload-delta cosine.
The effective delta is `B_upload A_upload − B_global_before A_global_before`,
compared jointly over all LoRA layers. The first stratum requires both clients
to have the indicated rank; the second requires one endpoint at that rank.
Domain means the recorded dominant category, not identical task mixtures.

| Total rank | Pair counts (rank / domain) | R0 gap | R1 gap | R2 gap | R9 gap |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 6 / 1 | −0.08873199 | +0.42494642 | +0.34977508 | +0.02748188 |
| 64 | 6 / 1 | −0.06623587 | +0.03701924 | −0.00441057 | +0.02274394 |

Both gaps are negative at R0, the first completed local round. Rank 16 is
positive at R1–R9; rank 64 is positive at R1/R9 and negative at R0/R2–R8.
Each group has six eligible same-rank/different-domain pairs per round.
The sole same-domain/different-rank reference is clients 3/5 (entailment),
and both curves subtract that same cosine. This is a sparse domain reference,
and the curves are not independent repeats or independent domain-effect estimates.
All selected cosines are valid in every round. The full pair table has 28
unordered pairs/round and 280 records over ten rounds; the figure has 20 rank/round points.

The import audit checks the 72 client metric records across the nine widths
against saved full-precision evaluations, and recomputes the 20 cosine points
from the 280 raw pair records with fixed rank/domain membership.
The performance and geometry s8 configs differ scientifically only in the
snapshot schedule; their final states are not claimed to be bitwise identical.
Both paper assets match their archived sources exactly.

The two studies are single-seed descriptive evidence. Neither varies partner
choice or reception strength, and cosine does not measure a donor's task effect.
They motivate global-width selection and fine-grained collaboration, without
establishing an allocation-by-collaboration interaction, a causal rank rule,
the need for dynamic control, or method superiority. The Problem's coupling
argument concerns how a receiving subspace and the applied external influence
constrain one another; its implementation and performance require separate evidence.

The retained Method operator remains a separate protocol: complete uploads,
a pooled target excluding self, projection of the full BA residual into native
receiving A rows, and modification of only the corresponding B columns.
It is still pending alignment with the Problem's global sharing definition.
The collaboration metric and dependent selection rules remain TODO.
The withdrawn §39 comparisons below retain their own fit/check protocol and
matched control; they do not evaluate the pending metric.

## Protocol and metric scope

- Qwen3.5-0.8B, seed 615, Dirichlet alpha 0.5, checkpoint R9 after 10 rounds (R0–R9). NI and GLUE have 16 clients with rank-group counts 6/5/5; MMLU-Pro has 13 clients with counts 5/5/3. The baseline runs end at R9; the withdrawn §39 rows evaluate R9 saved within the original 20-round training trajectories.
- NI and GLUE report client-macro ROUGE-L (%) from 200 generated test examples/client, using HF and the explicit chat-nothink template. GLUE uses the project causal generation protocol. MMLU-Pro reports client-macro logit MC accuracy (%) on every original test example/client; MC is not ROUGE-L.
- In NI, LocalOnly, FlexLoRA, FLoRA, and FedRoRA use 936 training examples/client with 64 held out. HetLoRA, PF²LoRA, pFedLoRA, and Het-CPFLoRA use the full 1000-example training pool. These are the saved protocols, not a claim of equal training-data or total-compute budgets.
- The withdrawn §39 NI group uses ni-4domain-16c-dir05-acval64: all 1000 local training examples plus 64 external validation examples/client. Calibration uses fit8 and confirmation uses check16; the remaining 40 validation examples are unused for formal selection. LocalOnly (matched) is the local arm of this same batch. Ours (soft) is the original controller; Ours (drop-veto variant) changes only the confirmation-drop-veto flag. The saved allocation schedule, including the original lock round 14, is retained when evaluating R9; these are not newly retrained 10-round schedules. Test data are used only for evaluation.
- PF²LoRA in this batch uses common rank 4 and private ranks 12/28/60, giving total ranks 16/32/64. This differs from the earlier native 4+2 runs. It uses exact hypergradients, common AdamW, and private SGD.
- Het-CPFLoRA uses shared ranks 12/24/48 and private ranks 4/8/16, with paper dynamic weighted inference. Calibration uses all 1000 local training prompts/client. Its generation score uses 200 test examples/client; the archived PPL uses only 100 examples/client and is an unweighted response-only diagnostic. The active score tables omit PPL.
- The configs set 10 local steps and effective batch 8. Ordinary runs use bs1/accum8; Het-CPFLoRA uses bs2/accum4. The base learning-rate field is 1e-4; pFedLoRA instead applies the saved private/auxiliary phase learning rates of 0.01. Method-specific phases and exact hypergradient work are not counted as an equal total compute budget.
- Values retain four percentage decimal places, matching the performance index. Fourteen baseline cells are recomputed from precise per-client eval_progress.json entries; MMLU-Pro FLoRA retains the published eval_results.csv average precision. The three withdrawn §39 cells are recomputed from the 16 precise client ROUGE-L entries in each metrics_full.json. Each dataset table lists only its available results; no old-rank or motivation-sweep score fills a missing result. Ranking marks apply within each current dataset table.
